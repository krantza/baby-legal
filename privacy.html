Privacy policy

Baby for iPhone · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: what you log stays yours. It is stored on your phone and in a database in Sweden, used only to show you your own entries and patterns, never sold and never used for advertising. You can export or delete everything from the app at any time.

Who is responsible

[NAME] is the data controller for the personal data processed in Baby. Contact: [EMAIL].

What Baby stores

  • What you log: your baby's breastfeeds (length and side), bottles (amount and contents), sleep, diapers, weight and height, and the names you give each child in the app, with the time of each entry and your time zone.
  • Your course progress: which lessons you have completed, and when.
  • An anonymous account: a random identifier created when you start using the app, so your entries can be stored safely. Baby does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • On your phone only: that you have given consent and when, and your settings, such as whether reminders are on.

Entries about your baby's feeding, sleep, diapers and growth can reveal information about their health, which is a special category of personal data under the GDPR. We treat everything you log as health data.

Why, and on what legal basis

Your entries are used only to show them back to you: what you logged, overviews of your days, patterns in your own data and your place in the course. Patterns are calculated on your phone.

The legal basis is your explicit consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a) and article 9(2)(a)), which you give when you first open the app. Nothing is sent to our servers, and no account is created, before you have given it.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and in a database in Stockholm, Sweden, within the EU. The database is run for us by Supabase Inc. as our data processor, under a data processing agreement that includes the EU standard contractual clauses for any access from outside the EU. Data is encrypted on its way between your phone and the database, and the key to your account is kept in the iPhone Keychain.

Who can see it

  • Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Baby contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics.
  • Apart from our database provider, nobody receives your data unless the law requires it.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; their content does not pass through any server.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Settings → Delete all my data removes your account and all your entries from our database immediately and then from your phone. Copies in our database backups disappear within 7 days.

If you remove the app without deleting your data first, the entries remain in our database linked to an anonymous account that nobody can reach any more, and we cannot tell which entries were yours. Please delete your data in the app before you uninstall it.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Export my data gives you everything stored about you, in a machine-readable format.
  • Correction: change or delete entries directly in the app. For anything you cannot change there, contact us.
  • Erasure and withdrawing consent: Settings → Delete all my data. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the data protection authority where you live.

Who the app is for

Baby is for parents and other adults caring for a child, not for children. Only log for children you are responsible for.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Changes that affect how your data is used will be shown in the app before they apply, and will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
